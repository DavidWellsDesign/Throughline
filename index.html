    Screen-space volumetrics with light shafts that hold up at 60fps on a Steam Deck. Quality tiers included for low-end targets.

How it works

  1. 01

    Import the package

    Drop in the rig

    Add the Nightfall Atmosphere prefab to your scene. Your existing lights keep working.

  3. 03

    Pick a look

    Choose a preset, then push the sliders until it’s yours. Save it back out as your own preset file.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Which engine versions are supported?

Unity 2022.3 LTS and newer (URP and HDRP), Unreal Engine 5.3+, and Godot 4.2+. Older versions are on the archive page and still work, but only the current majors get fixes.

Can I use it in a commercial game?

Yes, on every tier, with no revenue cap and no royalty. The only thing you can’t do is resell Nightfall itself as an asset.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep the version you bought forever, plus every update in that major version.

What happens when v3 comes out?

Existing customers get an upgrade price, not full price. v2 keeps working and keeps getting security and compatibility fixes for a year after v3 ships.

Do you offer student or hobbyist pricing?

Email us from your school address and we’ll sort you out. If money is genuinely the blocker, say so — we’d rather you had it.

How do refunds work?

Reply to your receipt within 30 days. We refund the full amount, usually same day. You don’t need to justify it.
